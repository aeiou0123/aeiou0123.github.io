### Claim2Value: Financial AI Agent for Technical Claim Verification and Dynamic Valuation

* **Competition**: PKU Financial AI Agent Competition (Awarded Project)
* **Role**: Economic & Financial Reasoning Lead
* **GitHub Repository**: [aeiou0123/pku-financial-ai-agent](https://github.com/aeiou0123/pku-financial-ai-agent)

#### Overview
Developed an end-to-end AI agent workflow for 11 core listed firms in the humanoid robotics and embodied AI supply chain. Benchmarked 51 domain technical claims, tracing the transmission from technical specifications to productivity and gross margins, integrated into dynamic three-scenario valuation models.
