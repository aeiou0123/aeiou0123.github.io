I am a third-year undergraduate student in **Quantitative Economics and Finance** at the [Jinhe Center for Economic Research](https://jinhe.xjtu.edu.cn/), [Xi'an Jiaotong University (XJTU)](https://www.xjtu.edu.cn/). Previously, I completed my foundational training in the **Special Class for the Gifted Young (SCGY)** at Qian Xuesen College and the School of Mathematics and Statistics at XJTU.

My research interests lie at the intersection of:
* **AI Exposure & Market Structure**: Exposure measurement, AI agent integration, and firm-level strategic responses.
* **Empirical Industrial Organization**: Pricing, cost pass-through, market concentration, and antitrust dynamics.
* **Macroeconomics & Macro-Finance**: Monetary transmission mechanisms, business cycles, and credit-debt dynamics.

I am enthusiastic about combining microeconometric causal inference (e.g., continuous DID, causal machine learning) with computational modeling and AI-native workflows to answer consequential questions in economics and finance.
